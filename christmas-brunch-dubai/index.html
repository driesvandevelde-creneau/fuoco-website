About Double Christmas Brunch at Fuoco

Fuoco hosts two Christmas brunches at Dubai Creek Harbour: one on Christmas Eve and one on Christmas Day. Expect sharing starters, a main from the robata grill and dessert, paired with a house or premium beverage package.

Fuoco Bar & Restaurant is at Grand, Dubai Creek Harbour, Dubai. Open Sunday to Thursday 17.00–01.00, Friday 17.00–02.00 and Saturday 14.00–02.00. Call or WhatsApp +971 50 256 6425 or email info@myfuoco.com.

When is the Christmas brunch at Fuoco?

Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December 2026, from 19.00 to 23.00, and Christmas Day, Friday 25 December 2026, from 14.00 to 17.00.
