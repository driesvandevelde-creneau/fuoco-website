About Office Xmas Party at Fuoco

Bring the whole team to Fuoco this December. Choose a sit-down Office Xmas party or a reception-style party with canapés passed around.

Fuoco Bar & Restaurant is at Grand, Dubai Creek Harbour, Dubai. Open Sunday to Thursday 17.00–01.00, Friday 17.00–02.00 and Saturday 14.00–02.00. Call or WhatsApp +971 50 256 6425 or email info@myfuoco.com.

Good to know

How much is the office Christmas party?

349 DH per person for a minimum of 10 guests, or 249 DH per person for the reception-style party from 20 guests.

What is included?

A 4-course Christmas menu with mulled wine on arrival: dishes to share, a main from the fire, sides and desserts to share. Unlimited drinks packages can be added from 89 DH per person. The reception-style party is a selection of canapés passed around.
